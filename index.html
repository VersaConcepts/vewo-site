What We Don't Do

No tactics. No hacks.

The AI visibility category is full of tactics Google has publicly debunked. We don't sell them.

✕

No llms.txt files. Google has stated these have no effect on AI search visibility.

✕

No content chunking for AI. Google has confirmed AI systems understand full pages and don't need content broken into machine-sized pieces.

✕

No rewriting content for AI extraction. Google has been explicit that content written for humans performs best, including in AI search.

✕

No mention farming or inauthentic citations. Google's systems detect and discount manufactured authority signals.

✕

No schema as a strategy. Structured data is foundational hygiene, not a path to AI recommendations.

We operate from Google's documented guidance on optimizing for generative AI search, not the hacks circulating in the AEO and GEO space. Read Google's official guidance →
