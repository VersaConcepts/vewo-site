The New Audience

Your website has a new audience: AI agents.

People were never your only visitors, and now they aren't even your most important ones. Before a buyer ever reaches you, an AI agent reads your site to decide whether to recommend you. It reads very differently than a person does, and if your content only speaks to humans, the agent moves on to a competitor it can understand.

How people experience your site

  • Skim headlines, images, and design
  • Click around and explore at their own pace
  • Forgive vague or messy pages
  • Judge you on look and brand feel
  • Visit one page at a time
  • Remember you and come back later

How AI agents read your site

  • Parse the text, structure, and meaning
  • Extract facts and claims they can cite
  • Need clear, well-structured answers
  • Judge you on clarity, authority, and trust
  • Read across your whole site at once
  • Decide in an instant whether to recommend you

A person will forgive a vague page and keep looking. An AI agent just recommends someone it understands better. VEWO helps make sure the content on your site will work for both.
