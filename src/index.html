How search changed

Traditional search→AI search→AI agents

Search has moved through three eras. Traditional search rewarded keywords and links. AI search answers a whole question in one place. Now AI agents research and shortlist brands on a person's behalf. The pattern is simple: discovered in AI, bought on your site.

AI breaks a single question into dozens of smaller ones, such as fit, materials, durability, and safety, before it decides which brands to recommend.

The brands that get recommended are the ones that answered those questions clearly and consistently. The problem is time: ads, launches, email, and support always come first, and content slips. VEWO keeps it moving.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does VEWO actually do?

We research what your customers ask Google and AI, then write, review, and publish 20 pieces of content a month on your site. Every 90 days we measure what changed and plan the next sprint.

What's in the free Visibility Report?

Where your brand shows up in Google and AI today, the questions your customers are asking, your biggest content gaps, and where competitors are ahead. It's yours to keep, whether or not you work with us.

Can I see sample content?

Yes. Our blog has published examples of the kind of work we do, and we're happy to walk you through a sample on a call.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything we publish lives on your site and belongs to you, even if you stop working with us.

Is there a contract?

Work runs in 90-day sprints. After each sprint you can continue, pause, or stop.

How fast will I see results?

Some changes show up within the first sprint. The larger gains come from consistent coverage over several sprints, since each one builds on the last.

How much work does my team have to do?

Very little. You share context up front and approve content. We handle the research, writing, and publishing.

Is every piece human-reviewed?

Yes. AI helps us work at scale, and a person edits every piece for accuracy, usefulness, and brand voice before it goes live.

Does VEWO work with Shopify, Webflow, or WordPress?

Yes. We publish within the site you already use. No migration and no new CMS.

Do you handle distribution?

We handle everything on your site, and we prepare LinkedIn, Reddit, and social assets for your priority content. Your team posts them.
