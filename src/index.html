The Content Playbook Changed

You used to optimize for searches. Now you have to prepare for conversations.

For years, content strategy was straightforward: find the keywords, create the pages, optimize for Google, build authority, try to rank. That still matters. But it isn't enough anymore. AI changed how customers search, and dramatically expanded the number of questions your brand needs to be able to answer.

Search used to hand customers a list of places to look. AI does the research for them and surfaces a much smaller set of answers and brands. If your brand isn't in that set, you're not in the consideration set.

Before

SEO was built around keywords

"best dog seat cover"

Keyword → Ranking → Click

Now

AI starts with a question, then asks many more

"What's the best dog seat cover for a large SUV with two dogs that shed heavily, protects the doors, doesn't slide around, and still lets me use one rear seat?"

Questions → Subquestions → Sources → Answers → Recommendations

Keywords aren't gone. The opportunity got bigger.

Traditional SEO asked what people are searching for. AI visibility adds another question: what could someone ask about this problem? That changes how content needs to be planned.

Then

  • Keywords
  • Search volume
  • Blog posts
  • Ranking pages
  • Traffic

Now

  • Keywords and prompts
  • Customer and AI fan-out questions
  • Comparisons and use cases
  • Buying criteria and FAQs
  • Product and service pages
  • Structured answers and entity clarity
  • Supporting evidence and citations

It's no longer enough to publish an article and move on. Your website needs to build complete coverage of the questions surrounding your business.

And AI needs content it can understand.

AI systems retrieve pieces of information, not simply webpages. What you say matters. How you structure it matters just as much.

  • Direct answers
  • Descriptive headings
  • Focused sections
  • Clear entities and relationships
  • FAQs and comparisons
  • Internal links between related topics
  • Structured data and schema
  • Specific evidence and expertise

The easier your content is to understand, extract, and trust, the easier it is for AI to use it when answering a customer's question.

VEWO consistently monitors what your customers and AI are asking, prioritizes the gaps, creates and human-reviews authoritative content, optimizes it for SEO, AEO, and GEO, publishes it to your site, and measures results, then reprioritizes every 90 days.

SEO helps customers discover your brand through traditional search. AEO creates authoritative answers built around the questions customers ask. GEO structures clear, useful information generative AI systems can understand, retrieve, cite, and surface. VEWO treats authoritative content as the shared foundation for all three.

Neither, and both. Software shows data and leaves execution to you. A traditional agency is human-heavy and high-touch. VEWO is a managed, AI-enabled, human-reviewed service focused on execution with a low workload for your team.

No. VEWO creates buying guides, comparisons, FAQs, category and collection content, product and service education, editorial content, and improvements to existing pages. The mix is determined by opportunity, not a fixed content quota.

VEWO owns the visibility foundation you control: your website, its content, structure, and on-site signals, created, human-reviewed, optimized, and published end to end. Yes. AI provides the scale; humans provide the judgment. Every publishable piece is reviewed for accuracy, usefulness, brand fit, and quality before it goes live.
