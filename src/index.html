FAQ

The questions growth-stage teams actually ask before handing over execution.

What does VEWO actually do?

VEWO consistently monitors what your customers and AI are asking, prioritizes the gaps, creates and human-reviews authoritative content, optimizes it for SEO, AEO, and GEO, publishes it to your site, and measures results, then reprioritizes every 90 days.

What are SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO helps customers discover your brand through traditional search. AEO creates authoritative answers built around the questions customers ask. GEO structures clear, useful information generative AI systems can understand, retrieve, cite, and surface. VEWO treats authoritative content as the shared foundation for all three.

How much does VEWO cost?

VEWO Managed Visibility is $2,995/month, run as continuous 90-day sprints. It delivers managed execution at a fraction of the cost and operational burden of building the capability in-house or using a traditional high-touch agency.

Does VEWO work with Shopify, Webflow, or WordPress?

Yes. VEWO works within the site you already use, including Shopify, Webflow, and WordPress. No migration and no new CMS.

How much work does my team have to do?

Very little. You give us context up front, then VEWO runs the execution. No dashboards to learn, no content calendar to manage, no writers to chase. Your team runs the business while VEWO keeps the visibility work moving.

Is VEWO software or an agency?

Neither, and both. Software shows data and leaves execution to you. A traditional agency is human-heavy and high-touch. VEWO is a managed, AI-enabled, human-reviewed service focused on execution with a low workload for your team.

Is VEWO just a blog-writing service?

No. VEWO creates buying guides, comparisons, FAQs, category and collection content, product and service education, editorial content, and improvements to existing pages. The mix is determined by opportunity, not a fixed content quota.

Do you handle distribution and promotion?

VEWO owns your on-site content end to end: create, human-review, optimize, and publish. For priority content, we also prepare aligned LinkedIn, Reddit, and social assets so your team has ready-to-publish material to amplify the work. We don't claim to automatically execute all off-site distribution.

Is every piece of content human reviewed?

Yes. AI provides the scale; humans provide the judgment. Every publishable piece is reviewed for accuracy, usefulness, brand fit, and quality before it goes live.

What is Brand Brain?

Brand Brain is the context layer VEWO builds about your business: products, customers, positioning, brand voice, competitors, existing content, expertise, proof points, and prior VEWO work. The longer VEWO works with your brand, the more context it has when deciding what to create next.

Why does this run in 90-day sprints?

Search, customer questions, competitors, and existing content all change. VEWO runs in continuous 90-day sprints so priorities get reset and reprioritized regularly. It's an ongoing operating model, not a one-time three-month project.
