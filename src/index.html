What Actually Changed

The rules of discovery quietly flipped.

Three shifts, and most brands haven't caught up to any of them.

From keyword matching to real questions

People ask AI what they'd ask a knowledgeable friend, in full sentences, with context. The old game of matching keywords doesn't describe how anyone searches anymore.

From ten links to one recommendation

Being #3 on a results page used to mean something. When there's one answer, you're in it or you're invisible. There is no page two.

From your website to the whole internet

AI checks what Reddit, YouTube, review sites, and the press say about you before recommending you. Your site is only one of the places it looks.

Our Belief

We've read the studies,
not just the sales pages.

Here's what we found, and it's simpler than most of what you'll hear elsewhere.

Google says the tricks don't matter

Google has said directly that you don't need llms.txt files, don't need to chunk content for AI, and don't need special schema to appear in AI answers. Usefulness and real, first-hand content matter more than any technical trick.

Source: Google Search Central, "Optimizing your website for generative AI features" (read it)

Real substance is what wins

Independent research found pages with real quotes, statistics, and cited sources gained 30 to 40% more AI visibility. Keyword stuffing and "authoritative-sounding" writing did nothing, and actually hurt results on some engines.

Source: Princeton and Georgia Tech GEO study, arXiv:2311.09735

Most of it happens off your site

What correlates most strongly with AI visibility isn't on your website at all. It's genuine mentions across the platforms AI checks: Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, reviews, and the press.

Source: Ahrefs, 75,000-brand study

So we don't sell schema audits as a growth strategy, "AI-optimized" file formats, or mention-buying. We do two things, consistently: answer the real questions your buyers are asking, on your site, and give you the structure to build genuine presence everywhere else AI is checking. That's the whole method.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

The questions operators actually ask before committing to a cycle.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO chased a position on a results page. We build for being the answer AI actually gives, which depends less on ranking and more on whether AI understands you, trusts you, and has seen you mentioned in the right places.

Isn't this just AEO or GEO with a new name?

No. A lot of what's sold as AEO/GEO right now is schema tricks, AI-specific file formats, and content chopped up for machines. Google has said none of that is necessary. We run the two things the evidence actually supports: real content that answers real questions, and a real distribution structure for the places AI is checking.

How long until I see results?

Weeks for first visible movement, quarters for presence that's hard for a competitor to catch up to. Anyone promising AI ranking gains in two to four weeks is selling you something the measurement itself can't support day to day.

What do I actually get every 90 days?

An audit, a written plan, on-site content researched, written, and published on schedule, a distribution structure and assets built for your team to run, and a clear read-out on what moved and what's next.

Do you handle our distribution and promotion for us?

We build the structure: platforms, messaging, cadence, order of operations, and the assets to support it. Your team runs the actual posting and outreach. We refine that structure every 90 days based on what's working.

Do I need to be technical to work with VEWO?

No. You need a business worth recommending. We handle translating that into what AI needs to see, hear, and trust.

What size company is this built for?

Brands with a real product and point of view, ready to commit to more than one 90-day cycle. This compounds; it doesn't work as a one-off.
