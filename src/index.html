The Change

Your website has a new audience: AI agents.

Buyers increasingly ask AI the questions they used to ask search engines, salespeople, and review sites. AI synthesizes an answer and recommends a handful of brands.

They ask AI first

ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, and Perplexity are now where research starts. Customers describe their problem in plain language and expect a direct recommendation.

AI decides who gets named

Instead of ten links, AI returns one synthesized answer naming a few brands. If AI can't clearly understand and trust you, it recommends someone it can.

Your signals have to be clear

AI has to grasp who you are, what you do, why you're credible, and which customer questions you can answer. Most websites never make that clear enough to be chosen.
